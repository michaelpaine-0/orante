About Orante

The traditional Catholic liturgical year, in the calendar you already use.

Orante is a free traditional Catholic calendar. It follows the 1962 Roman Missal and the 1960 Code of Rubrics, the calendar of the Traditional Latin Mass, and delivers the whole year as a free guide inside Apple Calendar, Google Calendar and Outlook. A day-by-day Ordo gives the detail behind each date.

What you get

Feasts
Every day's celebration with its class (I–IV), liturgical colour, Mass notes (Gloria, Credo, preface), commemorations, octaves, vigils, Ember and Rogation days.
Fasts
Fast and abstinence days under the 1917 Code of Canon Law (cc. 1250–1254): Lent, Ember days, vigils, Fridays. A reminder arrives the evening before.
Holy days
Holy days of obligation: the universal list of ten (1917) and the six kept in the United States, marked apart. Reminder the evening before.
Indulgences
Plenary indulgences tied to dates, from the Enchiridion Indulgentiarum (1999), with the usual conditions.
All
Every layer in one calendar.

Feeds are standard iCalendar files (.ics). They refresh on your provider's schedule and update by themselves when new years are published. 2026 is live; 2027 follows after review.

Sources and method

  • Calendar and precedence: Missale Romanum 1962; Rubricae Generales and the Code of Rubrics (1960), including the table of precedence, transfers and occurrence.
  • Movable feasts: computed from the date of Easter (Gregorian computus) each year.
  • Fast and abstinence: 1917 Codex Iuris Canonici, cc. 1250–1254. These are stricter than current US norms; Orante shows the older discipline and says so.
  • Holy days: 1917 CIC c. 1247 (universal list); the US indult list of six.
  • Indulgences: Enchiridion Indulgentiarum, 4th ed., 1999.

Orante is a private initiative, not an organ of any diocese or society. Where a local Ordo or your pastor differs, follow them. See the disclaimer.

How to add the free guide

  1. Open orante.net and press Start.
  2. Pick Everything, or choose layers.
  3. Tap Apple, Google or Outlook.

Apple shows it in about a minute. Google may take up to 12 hours to show events and refreshes about once a day. Outlook opens a page; press Import; it refreshes within about 12 hours. Direct feed links: All · Feasts · Fasts · Holy days · Indulgences.

How to remove Orante from your calendar

Orante is a calendar you added, not an app, so there is nothing to uninstall. Remove the calendar and every Orante event goes with it. Nothing is sent to us.

iPhone or iPad
Open Calendar, tap Calendars at the bottom, tap the ⓘ next to the Orante calendar, scroll down and tap Delete Calendar. If it does not appear there: Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Subscribed Calendars, tap Orante, Delete Account.
Mac
Open Calendar, right-click (or Control-click) the Orante calendar in the sidebar, choose Unsubscribe.
Google Calendar
On the web, hover over Orante under Other calendars in the left column, click the × (Unsubscribe). The phone app cannot remove it; use a browser.
Outlook (web)
In the left column, right-click the Orante calendar and choose Remove. If it is only hidden, untick it first, then right-click.
Outlook (desktop)
Right-click the Orante calendar in the folder list, choose Delete Calendar.
Android with Google
Removing it in Google Calendar on the web removes it from the phone too.

Added several layers? Each shows as its own calendar (Orante · Feasts, Orante · Fasts, and so on); remove each one. The Ordo keeps one setting in your browser; clear it under your browser's site data.

Questions

Is it free?
Yes. No accounts, no cookies of our own, no analytics. Your calendar app fetches the file directly. See privacy.
Is this the Novus Ordo calendar?
No. Orante follows the 1962 Missal used at the Traditional Latin Mass. Dates and ranks differ from the 1969 calendar.
Why do fasts look stricter than my parish bulletin?
Orante shows the 1917 discipline, which many who attend the traditional Mass keep by choice. Current binding law is lighter; the feed says which is which.
Can I use it outside the United States?
Yes. Holy days carry both the universal list and the US list; feasts and fasts are universal.
How do I remove it?
Delete or unsubscribe the Orante calendar in your calendar app; see above. Every event goes with it.
Something looks wrong.
Write to hello@orante.net with the date and the source you are checking against.

The name

Orante, from the Latin orans, is the ancient figure at prayer with hands raised, found in the catacombs. The site opens on the Elevation at a Pontifical Solemn High Mass, the moment the whole year turns on.
