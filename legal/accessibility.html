Accessibility

Effective 16 October 2026

We want Orante to be usable by everyone. The home page is a visual experience; everything it offers is also reachable without it.

Without motion or a mouse

  • The calendar can be added directly: all layers, feasts, fasts, holy days, indulgences.
  • The day-by-day Ordo at orante.net/app is a plain, keyboard-navigable page.
  • On the home page, arrow keys, Page Up/Down and the space bar move between stops; the Start button and the dialog work with Tab, Enter and Escape.

Standards

We aim for WCAG 2.1 AA: text contrast, visible focus, labelled controls, and a dialog that traps focus and returns it. The cursor reveal is decorative and carries no information.

Tell us

If something blocks you, write to us with the page and the assistive technology you use.
