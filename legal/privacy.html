Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026

The short version

Orante is a static website and a set of calendar files. It has no accounts, no sign-up, no forms, no cookies set by us, and no analytics or advertising scripts. We do not collect, store, or sell personal information.

What is processed, and by whom

  • Hosting. The site and calendar feeds are served by GitHub Pages. GitHub's servers see your IP address and request details in ordinary server logs, under GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive those logs.
  • Fonts. Pages load typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser sends a request to Google, which is covered by Google's privacy policy.
  • Scripts. The home page loads two open-source libraries (three.js, GSAP) from the cdnjs content-delivery network run by Cloudflare. That request is covered by Cloudflare's policy.
  • Calendar feeds. When you subscribe, your calendar provider (Apple, Google, Microsoft, or another) fetches the feed file on your behalf, on its own schedule. What that provider stores is governed by its policy, not ours. We receive nothing from it.

Local storage

The day-by-day Ordo at orante.net/app remembers the month and options you last viewed in your own browser's local storage. That data never leaves your device, and you can clear it with your browser's site-data controls.

Children

The site is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete. Requests about hosting or font logs should go to GitHub or Google respectively.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date.
