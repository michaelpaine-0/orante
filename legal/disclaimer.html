Devotional aid, not authority

Orante is a private aid for the faithful who follow the 1962 Missal. It does not bind anyone, replace a published diocesan or religious ordo, or stand in for the judgment of a priest or confessor.

Sources used

  • Calendar and rubrics: Rubricae generales (1960) and the Missale Romanum (1962), with United States proper feasts.
  • Fast and abstinence: Code of Canon Law 1917, canons 1250–1254. These are stricter than the norms now in force in the United States and are presented as a voluntary discipline.
  • Holy days of obligation: the 1917 list of ten, with the six binding in the United States marked.
  • Indulgences: Enchiridion Indulgentiarum, 4th edition (1999), and the Apostolic Penitentiary decree on Divine Mercy Sunday (2002). Conditions are summarized; the Enchiridion governs.

Known limits

  • Local calendars (diocese, religious order, parish titular feasts) are not included.
  • Transfers of impeded feasts are computed by rule; local ordos may differ.
  • Fasting law under the 1917 Code admits exemptions for age, health, labour, and state of life that the site cannot apply to you.
  • The calendar is reviewed year by year; errors may remain.

Health

Nothing here is medical advice. Anyone with a health condition, who is pregnant or nursing, or under the care of a physician should consult that physician and a confessor before fasting.
